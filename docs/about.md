# About

![Christian Bai](assets/headshot.jpeg){ .cb-portrait }
I'm a cybersecurity student and a working penetration tester based in Spotsylvania, VA, available for remote work.

Most of what I do is offensive security. I run authorized penetration tests against web apps and network services: scope the engagement, find vulnerabilities, prove it, write it up. The write-up matters as much as the finding, since a vulnerability nobody understands doesn't get remediated.

My coursework goes directly into the work rather than being useless. Offensive security, network and wireless security, cryptography, and cloud security all show up in what I actually do day to day. The legal and regulatory material sets the boundaries for what a test is allowed to touch and the criminology courses have turned out more useful than expected. Most breaches come down to someone making a bad call under pressure, not some novel exploit.

## How I work

I am most useful on engagements where the goal is depth rather than coverage.
I would rather fully understand one exploitable path and document it properly
than produce a scanner export with two hundred low-severity items in it.

I also try to stay honest about the limits of a test. A finding I could not
reproduce goes in the report as exactly that. Overstating a result costs you
credibility the first time somebody checks your work.
