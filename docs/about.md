# About

<img src="assets/headshot.jpg" alt="Christian Bai" class="cb-portrait">

I am a cybersecurity student and a working penetration tester based in
Fredericksburg, Virginia, and available for remote work.

Most of what I do falls on the offensive side of security. I run authorized
penetration tests against web applications and network services, which means
scoping the engagement, finding what is actually exploitable, proving it, and
then writing it up so the people who own the system can fix it. The write-up
matters as much as the finding. A vulnerability nobody understands does not get
remediated.

My coursework runs alongside that rather than separately from it. Offensive
security, network and wireless security, cryptography, and cloud security show
up directly in the work. The legal and regulatory material decides what a test
is permitted to do in the first place, and the criminology coursework I have
taken turns out to be more useful than it sounds, because most breaches come
down to a person making a decision under pressure rather than a novel exploit.

## How I work

I am most useful on engagements where the goal is depth rather than coverage.
I would rather fully understand one exploitable path and document it properly
than produce a scanner export with two hundred low-severity items in it.

I also try to stay honest about the limits of a test. A finding I could not
reproduce goes in the report as exactly that. Overstating a result costs you
credibility the first time somebody checks your work.
