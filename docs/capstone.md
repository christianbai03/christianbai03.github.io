## How the pieces connect

The program covered offensive security, networking, cryptography, cloud, law
and ethics, and social science. Those look unrelated on a transcript and are
not in practice.

An assessment is bounded by law before it is bounded by technique, since the
authorization defines what you are permitted to touch. Severity depends on
network design, because what a compromised host reaches determines what the
finding is worth. And whether a finding gets fixed depends on the people
receiving it, which is a human problem rather than a technical one.

## Notes on writing this

A few things that separate a reflection that reads well from one that reads
like it was written the night before.

**Use specifics.** "I improved my technical writing" is unverifiable. "My
first vulnerability write-up was three pages and the developer still had to
ask me what to change, so I rewrote it as half a page with the request and
the fix" is a claim with evidence attached.

**Include something that went wrong.** Reflection is a genre that rewards
candor. A mistake you diagnosed is more impressive than a success you
narrated.

**Write it in your own voice.** This is the one page on the portfolio where a
reader is explicitly looking for the person rather than the skill set.
