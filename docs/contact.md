## A note on the contact form

Your Wix site had a working contact form. A static site on GitHub Pages cannot
have one on its own, because there is no server behind it to receive the
submission.

If you want the form back, [Formspree](https://formspree.io/) and
[Netlify Forms](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/forms/setup/) both handle this
by taking the POST on their infrastructure and emailing you the result. Both
have free tiers. Formspree works on any host including GitHub Pages, so it is
the one that fits here.

That said, a form is not obviously worth it for a portfolio. A recruiter who
wants to reach you will use the email address, and a visible address is one
less step than a form. The form mainly buys you spam protection for your
address, which matters more if you are publishing under a professional domain
you intend to keep.
